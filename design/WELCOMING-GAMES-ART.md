Baghaduli prompt: Full-bleed landscape 3:2 cover. Same bold black ink outlines, warm painterly saturated colours and humorous Bangladeshi male caricatures as the reference. Three friends in shirts and lungis cheer over a traditional wooden Bagaduli board at a village tong: inverted-U top, inclined board, metal pegs, scoring pockets, glass marbles, narrow launch alley and plunger on the right. Tea glasses, biscuit jar, tin roofs, banana trees and pond. Welcoming turquoise, marigold yellow and coral. No lettering, logos, frame or TV.

Poker prompt: Full-bleed landscape 3:2 illustration, same bold outlines and Bangladeshi male caricatures. Four adult friends play a friendly card game at a rural tea stall. Playing cards, five community cards and colourful counters on teal cloth; tea glasses, biscuit jar, rickshaw, banana plants and pond. Rich turquoise, mustard, coral, green and cream. No cash, casino, western suits, signage, frame or TV.

Day edit prompt: Turn the same village tea-stall composition into cheerful sunlit daytime. Preserve stall, tin roofs, kettle, cups, jars, benches, courtyard, trees, pond, boat and distant houses. Bright cyan sky with clouds, fresh greens, straw, terracotta earth and warm sunshine. Unlit lantern, no window glow. Retain ink outlines and painterly shading, no added people, text or frames.
