# Bangladeshi cartoon artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 15 November 2026. This set replaces the earlier painterly covers with bold 2D cartoons of everyday Bangladesh: local clothing, tong tea stalls, village courtyards and school scenes. Kalidash is represented as a folk-riddle storyteller character.

Final files: `www/assets/<game>/cover-art.webp` (1200×800) and `cover-tile.webp` (600×400) for all twelve games. Kalidash portrait: `www/assets/kalidas/poet-art.webp` (960×960) and `poet-avatar.webp` (240×240). Only WebP conversion and Lanczos resizing were applied; no cropping or creative image edits. Source IDs and export sizes are recorded in `artwork-manifest.json`. Existing saved-history assets remain available.
