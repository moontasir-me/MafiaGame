# Consistent cartoon character artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 15 November 2026. This set contains 26 Mafia portraits, two Spy portraits and three universe covers, all matching the approved bold-outline, cel-shaded Bangladeshi game illustrations. Polashi preserves its Bengal historical roles; Sicily preserves its Italian cast; Classic and Spy use contemporary Bangladeshi neighbors. The Polashi villager is an unnamed farmer.

Production paths are recorded in [character-artwork-manifest.json](character-artwork-manifest.json). The twenty Polashi/Sicily portraits remain PNG files at `www/assets/bn/<role>.png` and `www/assets/en/<role>.png`; covers remain `www/assets/<world>/cover.png`. Classic portraits are embedded WebP images in the existing six-view `www/assets/classic/characters.svg` file. Spy portraits use self-contained SVG image wrappers at `www/assets/spy/agent.svg` and `detective.svg`. Existing URLs and Classic fragment IDs are retained so saved rankings update without migration.

Only resizing, PNG/WebP encoding and SVG packaging were applied to generated images. No creative edits or crops were applied outside the image-generation tool. Earlier SVG cover URLs and the Fibonacci illustration now wrap the approved cartoon covers, keeping old saved results consistent. UI icons, patterns and playable vector game pieces retain their functional vector forms.

## Art direction for future work

Use bold dark contour lines, readable exaggerated faces, rounded shapes, crisp cel shading, restrained printed-paper grain and teal/green, mustard, coral and cream accents. Bangladeshi scenes use local clothing, tong shops, glass tea cups, bamboo furniture and river/village settings. Preserve each Mafia universe's own setting and character identities. Avoid mixing realism, watercolor miniatures and flat icon placeholders into character art.
