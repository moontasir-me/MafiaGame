# Dhoni Hobar Mojar Khela artwork

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, using the existing Mafia cover as the illustration style reference. Original Bangladesh property-game artwork; no Richup assets used.

Production files:
- `www/assets/dhoni/cover-art.webp` — 1200 × 800
- `www/assets/dhoni/cover-tile.webp` — 600 × 400

Creation prompt: A landscape cartoon cover of three cheerful Bangladeshi friends in casual shirts and lungis playing a property-trading board game at a village tea stall. Bold clean black outlines, warm painted colours, expressive friendly male faces, unmistakably Bangladesh. A colourful board, two dice, miniature wooden houses, a hotel and stylized green taka notes. A whimsical backdrop evokes Dhaka apartments, a river launch, rickshaw, tea garden and Cox’s Bazar. Jewel green, mustard, terracotta and cream. No text, logos, exact banknotes, watermark or Indian architectural symbols.

Final editing prompt: Remove the entire outer television case, knobs, grille, bezel, black inner frame and wooden border. Only the full-bleed cartoon scene, extending to all four image edges. Preserve the three friends, tea-stall setting, miniature property board, houses, hotel, two dice, stylized taka notes, river launch, beach and tea garden; same bold inked style, cheerful faces and warm colours. Clean landscape 3:2 composition. No TV, frame, border, lettering or UI.

Production preparation: Pillow Lanczos resizing, WebP quality 88, encoded in memory and verified after writing. The TV enclosure is supplied by the existing home-page CSS.
