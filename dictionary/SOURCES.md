# Spy Bengali word pools

The extended pool contains 20,000 unique NFC-normalized Bengali-script word forms, in frequency order, adapted from Hermit Dave's FrequencyWords / OpenSubtitles2018 Bengali bn_50k.txt: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/bn/bn_50k.txt . The source content is CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); this adapted extended pool is distributed under the same licence. Source licence declaration: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords . Changes: normalize Unicode, remove non-Bengali tokens, numbers, very long tokens, duplicates, and a basic explicit-term blocklist, then retain the first 20,000.

Frequency is not a guarantee of familiarity: the pool includes inflections, names, spelling variants, and transliterations from subtitles. It is not advertised as 20,000 universally familiar dictionary headwords or fully human-reviewed. The recommended everyday pool is a separately curated list of concrete foods, household items, animals, places and activities, used by default. Hosts can choose the extended pool or enter their own Bengali word. Further local playtesting and word reports can improve coverage.
